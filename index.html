Live clipping

clips while the stream is on
Records the stream, watches chat speed and cuts a clip around every chat spike (starting ~24 s before it). Uses your layout, facecam boxes, caption style and language from Create clips. Works only while the stream is live.

Creators

add a streamer once, clips start by themselves when they go live
fastclipping checks every minute. When a creator on your list goes live, it records the stream and clips the chat spikes with the layout, facecam boxes and caption style you had selected when you added them (press “Use my current layout” to change it later). One stream at a time. Keep fastclipping open; unattended recordings stop after 3 hours (a new one starts if they are still live), and the big recording file is deleted after the clips are cut.

Find moments with a chatbot

A chatbot given a 9-hour transcript only really reads the start. So: split the transcript into small parts, paste each part into any free chatbot (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude…), paste its answers back here, and fastclipping downloads only those sections and makes the clips. Needs a YouTube video with captions. Nothing is sent anywhere by fastclipping.

2 · Paste the chatbot answers (all parts together is fine)

Cloud AI (optional) faster titles and moment picking on a slow PC

Use any OpenAI-compatible provider (Groq, OpenRouter, Gemini, OpenAI, LM Studio). When this is on, only the text of a candidate clip (its transcript lines) is sent to your provider. Your video, audio and frames never leave this PC. The key stays in data/llm.json on this computer. Off by default.

Quick guide

Your next clip starts here.

From a long video to a moment worth sharing, in your own workspace.

  • Bring your video

    Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick replay link, or upload your own video.

  • Make it yours

    Choose your clip length, layout and caption style. Use Preview video for hands-on framing.

  • Find your moments

    Choose Make clips for automatic highlights, or review and export a selected range yourself.

  • Ready to share

    Review your captions, then download your finished exports from My clips.
